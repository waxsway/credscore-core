# CredScore (CRED)
Core logic and MVP source code for the CredScore decentralized reputation protocol.

**CredScore** is a Web3-based decentralized credit scoring and reward system designed to empower users through transparent, reputation-based financial scoring. By leveraging on-chain behavior, community validation, and reward incentives, CredScore aims to build the trust layer of the decentralized economy.

---

## 🚀 What is CredScore?

CredScore is a blockchain-native credit protocol that:
- Creates dynamic **on-chain credit scores** based on wallet activity, reputation, and behavioral data.
- Rewards good behavior with **$CRED tokens**, NFTs, and increased borrowing limits.
- Enables protocols, DAOs, and DeFi platforms to **assess user credibility** before offering services or access.

Whether you're a builder, a borrower, or a degenerate yield farmer, CredScore helps you prove you're *not just another anon with a MetaMask*.

---

## 💡 Why It Matters

Traditional credit systems are opaque, exclusionary, and don't work in Web3. CredScore flips the model:
- No centralized gatekeepers
- No invasive identity checks
- Just provable, on-chain reputation

Your Web3 actions *are* your credit history.

---

## 🧩 How It Works

1. **Data Aggregation**
   - Tracks wallet-level behavior (e.g. loan repayments, DAO votes, staking habits, etc.)
   - Integrates with DeFi protocols, DAOs, social graph data, and GitHub activity

2. **Score Calculation**
   - Uses a transparent scoring algorithm with weights for:
     - Financial responsibility (e.g. no rug pulls, loan defaults)
     - Community engagement (votes, contributions)
     - Trust signals (NFTs, POAPs, account age, etc.)

3. **Token Incentives**
   - Users earn $CRED for maintaining high scores and verifying contributions
   - Scores unlock tiers: **Standard**, **Trusted**, **Elite**, each with perks

---

## 🔐 Privacy First

No KYC. No doxxing. Just wallet-level pseudonymous reputation. All score logic and data sources are transparent and community auditable.
